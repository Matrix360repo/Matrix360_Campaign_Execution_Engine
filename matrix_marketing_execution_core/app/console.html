Help and operating limits

Why does my lead need review even with a high score?

A score explains business fit. A reviewer checks the evidence and makes the qualification decision. It does not establish consent or guarantee a purchase.

Why is my eligible audience empty?

Check that the client has the right project profile, the lead has a qualified decision, the destination is valid, the channel permission is recorded and suppression is off. New imports after approval require a new campaign.

Does “completed” mean the message was delivered?

No. This release uses a simulation adapter. Completed means the rehearsal finished. Actual delivery requires evidence from an authorized provider.

What happens after a restart?

Pending tasks remain in the configured database and resume when the worker starts. Hosted staging refuses local SQLite; use PostgreSQL. Database backup and restore must be verified before a real pilot.

Are other Matrix 360 engines required?

No. Mayur data engine, Tanmayi creative engine and Avinash CRM are not runtime dependencies.

Can I send a live campaign now?

No. No live adapter or provider credentials are enabled. Provider authorization, consent evidence, delivery callbacks and explicit live campaign approval are required first.
